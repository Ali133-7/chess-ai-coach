Expanded local game library

Source: Lichess official broadcast archives, https://database.lichess.org/#broadcasts
Attribution: Lichess official broadcasts and the tournaments linked in each record.

Changes: selection of completed standard games with known player names and year;
validation of all recorded mainline moves; normalized dates; exact-identity
deduplication; compressed PGN and numerical source evaluations; search index.
Human annotations and side variations are not included. Bilingual board-fact
explanations are generated locally by the application, rather than copied from
members of another site. Source engine settings are unknown; missing evaluations
remain unscored. No separately computed deep analysis of every position is claimed.

The adapted game data and original board-fact game notes are shared under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Retain this attribution, license link and changes notice when
redistributing, and share adaptations under the same license.

The artifact's game-library-manifest.json identifies exact archive URLs, SHA-256
checksums, import scopes, record counts and exclusions. Coverage is limited to
those accepted records. This is not all chess games ever played and is not a
redistribution of the Chessgames.com archive.

Application source code is separately GPL-3.0-or-later. See ../LICENSE and
../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. No player accounts, payment records, service keys or
private training history are included in this public library.
